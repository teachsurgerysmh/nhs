Take & Post-Take Flow

The one cycle

Every shift — nights, weekends, twilights — is a different vantage point on this.

1 · Take

On-call day. You admit new patients to the Emergency GI Surgery: Take list.

2 · PTWR

Next day. The night team moves them to Post-take; you own them on the post-take ward round.

3 · Ward · 17:00

At 17:00 they leave your list and transfer to the ward team.

Weekend Take

Friday to Monday: one team, no daily alternation.

Friday: [Specialty] Team starts TAKE
↓
Admits patients Friday
↓
Saturday — SAME team
PTWR own patients + keep admitting new ones (no separate post-take team)
↓
Sunday — SAME team
PTWR own patients + keep admitting new ones
↓
Monday AMNew Take team starts fresh admissions
Monday AMWeekend team becomes Monday’s PTWR team for everyone accumulated
Need the OTHER specialty urgently mid-weekend? Contact them directly — don’t wait for Monday, there’s no alternation to ride over a weekend.
On Call and PTWR always run as a consecutive pair. Emergency operating: on-call covers 08:00–10:00 and 17:00–20:30; PTWR covers 10:00–17:00. On a weekend take you accumulate patients across the whole weekend onto Take, then move them to PTWR on the Monday.
The shape of it

Admit today. Review on the post-take round tomorrow.

What happens at 17:00 depends on the diagnosis: most go straight to the ward team, and only the ones that turn out to belong to the other specialty re-join that evening’s take. A weekend take doesn’t alternate at all — the same team accumulates patients from Friday through to Monday’s post-take round.
