EPISODE IV
A NEW SHO

Civil war has come to Gate 36. Rebel F1s, striking from a hidden office on 32B, face the full might of the BLEEP EMPIRE — switchboard 100, outside line 9, bleep 81. GI on call: Reg 9772, SHO 9770, F1 9662. Theatre co‑ordinator 1535. First anaesthetist 9031.

During the battle, rebel spies stole the secret plans to the Empire's ultimate weapon: THE FLOW. Take → PTWR → Ward. Admit today, own tomorrow, hand back at 17:00 — and who returns to Take, and who passes to the ward team, is always the question. On call holds the gates of Theatre 5 from 08:00 to 10:00, then returns 17:00–20:30; PTWR runs 10:00–17:00. SDEC closes to referrals at 17:00 — after that it goes to ED, or 32B.

They are not alone in the fight. CT‑3PO waits at Gate 18, speaking only in contrast phases. Obs‑Wan Kenobi warns that the NEWS has been climbing since dawn. Darth Bladder patrols urology, catheter drawn. And on 26A, Princess Ileus awaits a flatus that never comes.

Hunted through the corridors by the Empire's agents, a new cohort arrives at Southmead carrying the stolen plans — one handbook, custodian of everything they will need. Careflow lists on day one. CEPOD listed through Bluespier to the Brunel L2 Emergency Board. Every laparotomy scored on NELA; over 10% goes to MDT with ITU and anaesthetics before booking. And the last commandment, never broken: NO OPEN RETURNS. Every SDEC discharge gets a discharge summary — even the ones coming back tomorrow.

May the Flow be with you.
